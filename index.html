Two extra hours of tutoring

For students in Year 2 and above: one hour of English and one hour of Mathematics each week, through live online tutoring with recordings to revisit.

Students invited to a scholarship interview receive coaching on their resume and interview skills from an experienced scholarship mentor.

03

Guidance for parents, too

One enrolled-family seminar each term, sharing school selection insights, entrance exam preparation experience and education information.
